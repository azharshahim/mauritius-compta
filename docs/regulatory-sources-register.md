# Mauritius Payroll — Regulatory Sources Register

**Mission 15 / last access: 31 October 2026.**
This register separates four things that must never be conflated: a source was identified, its content was read, a formula was implemented, and an expert approved its applicability. **No rule below is approved by an expert accountant or by the MRA.** No legal compliance is claimed.

## Status vocabulary

| Field | Values used here |
|---|---|
| Source status | `SOURCE IDENTIFIED` → `CONTENT CHECKED` → `DIVERGENCE DOCUMENTED` |
| Implementation status | `NOT IMPLEMENTED`, `IMPLEMENTED`, `IMPLEMENTED + TESTED` |
| Applicability | `NOT REVIEWED` for every rule in this mission |
| Expert approval | `PENDING` for every rule in this mission |

A rule may be `IMPLEMENTED + TESTED` and still be `PENDING` for expert approval. The payroll engine never treats implementation as approval.

## Legal sources read but not applied to a payroll formula

| Topic | Source | Why it is not in the engine |
|---|---|---|
| Wages Regulation Orders 2026 (33 sectoral regulations) | https://labour.govmu.org/Pages/wages-2026.aspx | Sectoral minimum wages are outside this mission's statutory-contribution scope; no rate is stored. |
| WRA hours, overtime, leave, payslip | WRA 2019 consolidated v8 and https://labour.govmu.org | No legal premium, leave entitlement or overtime rate is invented; the engine records hours and emits warnings. |
| Companies Act 2001, Financial Reporting Act 2004, FRC, MIPA | See `docs/accounting-regulatory-index.md` (separate index) | Accounting index, not payroll. No IFRS is used for payroll. |

## Open points grouped for the single expert review

1. Confirm the PAYE 2026/27 bands and the exact treatment of the 13th (bonus) month in cumulative scaling.
2. Confirm whether CSG, NSF and Training Levy have a statutory rounding convention; currently exact to four decimals.
3. Confirm public-sector/PRB CSG rates and CSG Income Allowance treatment.
4. Confirm WRA s.54 bonus base, qualifying period, and interaction with PAYE/CSG/NSF; read EOYGA 2001.
5. Confirm AR 2026 eligibility for atypical workers and the piece-rate schedule.
6. Confirm the MRA Joint Monthly Return file specification and deadlines before generating any “ready to submit” file.
